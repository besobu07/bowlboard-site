# Owner review checklist

- Confirm the registered operating entity and contact information.
- Verify deployed Supabase RLS, account deletion RPC, backup retention, data residency, subprocessors and contracts. Client source alone does not establish backend behavior.
- Confirm whether any analytics, cookies, tracking or advertising are used beyond reviewed code.
- Verify local storage and photo deletion, including recovery copies and legacy embedded thumbnails.
- Confirm age policy and jurisdictions before launch; review league rosters that may contain minors’ data.
- Confirm pricing, payment provider, subscription model, cancellation and refunds before paid launch. No paid features are currently described as available.
- Add App Store-specific EULA terms and privacy disclosures once native packaging and SDKs are known.
- Obtain legal review, set effective date, remove review banners/noindex, then publish.
