Everything a league bowler needs

🎳

Pin-by-pin scoring

Tap the pins that fell. Strikes, spares, splits, and 10th-frame fill balls handled correctly.

📸

Photo score import

Snap the center's scoresheet and BowlBoard drafts the game for you to verify.

📊

Stats & trends

Average, high game, high series, strike% and spare% — charted over time, split by league or practice.

🗂️

History & filters

Every series logged with center, lanes, oil pattern, and ball. Find any night in seconds.

🎱

Ball arsenal

Track your equipment — brand, model, weight — and see which ball scores best for you.

🏆

League sheets

Keep the whole league's weeks in one place. Your standings, your average, your handicap.
