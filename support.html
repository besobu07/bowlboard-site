Frequently asked questions

Is BowlBoard free?

Yes — BowlBoard is free while in development, and no account is required. You can track unlimited games with a manual entry, with 5 photo scans per month during the beta.

How does photo import work?

Take a photo of the lane scoreboard (or choose one from your photos), box your row, and BowlBoard reads the frames and builds your scorecard. Frames it isn't sure about are flagged for you to review — nothing is saved without your confirmation.

Do I need an account?

No. BowlBoard works fully offline without one. An account lets your bowling history sync across devices — your data is local-first either way.

Where does my data go?

Your games stay on your device. If you create an account, a synced copy is stored in our secure database (Supabase), separated per user. Scoreboard photos always stay on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I get my old scores into BowlBoard?

In the app, go to More → Import my scores. You can import from a CSV or Excel file with a date and score per row, with a preview and duplicate detection before anything is added.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. More → Export my games gives you an Excel workbook, CSV, or printable PDF report. Your data is yours.

Does BowlBoard work offline?

Yes. Everything except photo OCR fallback and cloud sync works offline, including the offline glyph reader for photo import.
