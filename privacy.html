BOWLBOARD

Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

BowlBoard (“BowlBoard,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a bowling record and scoring application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, when it is stored locally or in the cloud, and the choices available to you.

1. Information you provide

Depending on how you use BowlBoard, you may provide:

  • Account information: email address and, if you choose, a display name when you create an account.
  • Bowling data: games, scores, frames, sessions, dates, bowling centers, lanes, equipment, oil patterns, league information, notes, and related records you choose to enter.
  • Photos: images of bowling scoreboards or score sheets when you use photo capture. In the current beta, these photos are stored on your device and are not included in ordinary cloud account sync.
  • Support information: information you choose to include when contacting us, such as your email address, device/browser information, and a description of a problem.

2. Information collected automatically

BowlBoard may receive limited technical information from hosting, authentication, and security providers, such as IP address, browser/device information, timestamps, and request information. We use this information as necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service.

BowlBoard is not designed to collect precise location information, contacts, health information, financial information, or advertising identifiers.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • provide scoring, history, statistics, insights, imports, backups, and other BowlBoard features;
  • authenticate accounts and maintain sessions;
  • sync your bowling data when you choose to use an account;
  • respond to support requests and diagnose problems;
  • protect the service against abuse, fraud, and security threats; and
  • improve BowlBoard and develop new features.

We do not use your bowling history to make decisions about you unrelated to BowlBoard.

4. Local-first storage

BowlBoard is designed to keep your bowling data on your device whenever practical. You can use the app without an account. In that mode, your games and related local data remain in browser/device storage unless you export, share, or otherwise transmit them.

5. Accounts and cloud storage

If you create a BowlBoard account, we use Supabase for authentication and cloud storage. Your cloud BowlBoard record is associated with your authenticated account and protected by row-level access controls. Cloud sync is intended to provide backup and access across devices.

In the current beta, scorecard photos are kept locally on the device rather than uploaded as part of the cloud sync record.

6. Third-party service providers

BowlBoard relies on service providers to operate parts of the product, including:

  • Supabase: account authentication and cloud data storage.
  • GitHub Pages / hosting providers: delivery of the web application and public website, as applicable.
  • Tesseract.js delivery infrastructure: the beta may download the text-recognition library from a third-party content delivery network. The OCR processing itself is performed in the browser; BowlBoard does not intentionally send your scoreboard image to that CDN.

These providers may process technical information necessary to provide their services. We do not sell your personal information to these providers.

7. Sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may disclose information to service providers that process it on our behalf, when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or property of BowlBoard, our users, or others.

8. Your choices and deletion

You may use BowlBoard without creating an account. If you create an account, you can sign out, request a password reset, and use the account deletion feature in the app. Account deletion removes your cloud account and associated cloud BowlBoard data; local device data may remain unless you separately erase it from your device.

You may also contact us at hello@bowlboard.app to ask about access, correction, deletion, or other privacy questions. Where applicable law gives you additional rights, we will honor those rights subject to lawful exceptions and verification requirements.

9. California privacy rights

California law may provide qualifying residents with rights concerning access, deletion, correction, and certain disclosures of personal information. BowlBoard does not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising. Where applicable, you may exercise privacy rights by contacting hello@bowlboard.app. We may need to verify your request before completing it.

10. Data retention

We retain account and cloud data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When an account is deleted, BowlBoard is designed to delete the associated cloud application data and authentication account, subject to limited backups or records that may need to be retained for legitimate purposes.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the information we process. No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

12. Children

BowlBoard is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at hello@bowlboard.app.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as BowlBoard changes. We will post the revised policy at this location and update the effective date.

14. Contact

BowlBoard is operated by BowlBoard, LLC.

Privacy questions: hello@bowlboard.app
