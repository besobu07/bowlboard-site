About this policy

BowlBoard (“BowlBoard,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides bowling scorekeeping and history tools through its website and web application (the “Service”). This policy explains the information we handle, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we handle

Depending on how you use the Service, we may handle account information (email, optional display name, authentication and session information); bowling records (scores, frames, equipment, dates, centers, lanes, league rosters and related entries); scoreboard photos you select; and information you voluntarily provide to support. Bowling records and photos may include other people’s names and scores. Our hosting, authentication and content delivery providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser or device details and request timestamps. The current Service does not request precise GPS location, contacts, health information or payment card details.

2. How information is used

We use information to provide scoring, recognition, history, statistics, league management, backups and account synchronization; authenticate users; maintain and secure the Service; troubleshoot and improve features; respond to support requests; and comply with applicable law. Where a legal basis is required, we rely on providing the requested service, legitimate interests in operating and securing it, consent where required, or legal obligations, as appropriate.

3. Local records and optional accounts

You may use scorekeeping without an account. The web app stores records and preferences in browser storage and may keep recent recovery copies. Photo thumbnails are ordinarily stored separately in IndexedDB. Software files are cached for offline use. If you create an account and sign in, Supabase provides authentication and cloud storage. Existing local records may be uploaded when connecting an account, and local changes may sync automatically while signed in. If conflicting records exist, you may be asked which to keep. The synchronized record may contain league members’ information, photo references and any embedded image data in older or imported records. Photos stored separately in the local photo database are not ordinarily included in sync. Signing out does not necessarily delete local or cloud copies.

4. Camera, photos and recognition

When you choose to capture or import a photo, the app uses your device’s camera or file picker. The built-in glyph reader and Tesseract.js process scoreboard images in your browser. Recognition software and language resources may be downloaded from third-party content delivery services; ordinary OCR does not require uploading the image to a remote OCR service. A thumbnail of the selected original image may remain on your device even if you crop to a single player for recognition. Review photos and avoid including unnecessary personal information. Information you export, share through your device, or send to support may leave your device.

5. Service providers and disclosures

Supabase handles authentication and cloud records; GitHub Pages and associated infrastructure host the website and web app; jsDelivr and related infrastructure deliver software resources. Providers process information necessary to deliver their services and their own privacy policies may apply to their independent activities. We may disclose information where required by law, to protect security or safety, to address misuse, or during a business transfer subject to applicable safeguards. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. The current Service is not designed for targeted advertising.

6. Cookies and browser storage

The Service uses browser storage, cached files and authentication session mechanisms to save records, support offline use and maintain sign-in. Blocking or clearing these mechanisms may prevent features from working or erase local data. Service providers may also use technical mechanisms necessary to deliver and secure their services.

7. Future premium features

The current Service is free to start and does not offer paid subscriptions or collect payment card details. Optional paid features may be introduced later. Before introducing payments, we will update our disclosures as appropriate to explain the billing providers and purchase-related data we receive. If an app store processes purchases, its privacy policy also applies to that processing.

8. Retention and deletion

Cloud records are retained as needed to provide the account and Service, and limited information may be retained as reasonably necessary for security, legal obligations, dispute resolution or backup recovery. To request account deletion, sign in and choose Delete account in account settings, or email hello@bowlboard.app if you cannot access the account. The app is designed to request deletion of your cloud account and associated cloud bowling record. Local history may remain. To erase local information, use the app’s data-management tools; the app may retain a recovery copy when erasing records. Clearing all site storage on each device removes remaining browser-stored copies, but also removes other saved records and settings. Separately remove any exports or shared copies you no longer wish to keep. Browser storage can be evicted, so export important records before erasing or changing devices.

9. Privacy choices and rights

You may use the Service without an account, sign out, reset your password, export your records, and request access, correction or deletion by contacting hello@bowlboard.app. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may also have rights to portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, appeal or complaint to a supervisory authority. We may verify your identity before responding, and lawful exceptions may apply. California residents may have additional rights under applicable law, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising privacy rights. We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising.

10. Security and international processing

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information handled, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Service providers may process information outside your home jurisdiction. Where required, applicable safeguards must be used for international transfers.

11. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Please avoid entering unnecessary identifying information about children in league records.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as features and practices change. We will publish the revised policy and its effective date and provide additional notice when required. Privacy inquiries and requests: hello@bowlboard.app. Proposed operator: BowlBoard, LLC, subject to confirmation of the legal entity before publication.
